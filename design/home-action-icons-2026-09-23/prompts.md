# Propuestas de iconos de la home — 23-09-2026

Creadas con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`, aprobadas e integradas en los botones Aventura y Partida rápida de la home. Sus copias de producción están en `assets/images/home/`; conservan los PNG RGBA originales. El atlas que contiene el icono de play permanece intacto y los demás botones siguen utilizándolo.

- `adventure-map.png`: propuesta original crema, conservada como referencia. La versión actual es `adventure-map-blue.png`: silueta azul simplificada; ver [mapa-azul.md](mapa-azul.md).
- `quick-play-bolt.png`: rayo azul con volumen suave, pensado para contrastar sobre el botón amarillo de Partida rápida.

Ambos archivos son PNG RGBA de 1254×1254. Se verificó que tienen alfa real, píxeles completamente transparentes y las cuatro esquinas con alfa cero. Copiados desde los originales sin convertir el formato ni perder el canal alfa.

## Prompt del mapa

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Requested individual asset: a FOLDED ADVENTURE MAP icon for the Aventura button of SunDoku. This will appear over a bright sky-blue button, so the warm ivory map must stand out clearly.
Draw one compact folded map with THREE broad accordion-fold panels, open and facing almost front-on, slightly tilted for charm. A thick soft cream-paper body, rounded corners, a subtle navy edge underneath for a readable silhouette, gentle golden tint on the folds. The overall silhouette should be close to square, approximately 1.12:1 width to height. On the ivory face include ONLY a simple thick navy curved trail crossing the three folds and ending at ONE small chunky golden location pin with a navy center near the upper-right area. A single quiet soft-green land shape on the lower-left fold is enough. Keep details bold and sparse; no fine grid, no tiny dotted trail, no intricate miniature landscape. All elements belong to the same single folded map object. Soft rounded thickness like SunDoku's existing play and settings icons.
Style: SunDoku, a cheerful children's sudoku game with soft sculpted 3D toy-like illustrations, rounded bevels, rich navy #082A62 accents, warm buttery gold, ivory cream and gentle upper-left highlights. Tactile subtly textured satin-gloss material, not flat vector art, not photoreal metal, not hard glass, not casino ornamentation. Very legible as a 34–40 logical pixel UI icon. Square 1024x1024 canvas, one centered subject occupying about 78% of the canvas, fully visible with clean safe margins. No letters, words, numbers, labels, borders around the canvas, badge, disc, button, scenery, ground plane, watermark, particles or external drop shadow.
Extract or generate only the requested individual reusable UI artwork, never a complete screen or a flattened modal. Keep modal, card, panel and button surfaces blank, with no text, lettering, captions, labels or variable values. All UI text will be rendered separately with Flutter Text widgets. Reuse existing yellow buttons, cards and panels; do not redraw them as part of this asset. Keep the artwork independent from layout so Flutter can center the content and place yellow action buttons in the bottom action area.
Genuine transparent RGBA PNG background. Every pixel outside the requested foreground artwork must have zero alpha, including gaps between elements. No opaque background, no painted checkerboard, no scenery, no replacement backdrop, no wide halo. Preserve the foreground artwork and its clean antialiased edges.

In that case use EXACTLY uniform #FF00FF, absolutely no checkerboard, texture, reflections, shadow or gradient in the background. Do not include magenta within the requested foreground artwork.
