## Prompt

Remove the background. Keep only the existing foreground artwork, isolated on genuine transparency. All gray and white checkerboard background pixels must have zero alpha, including the gaps around and inside the artwork. Preserve shapes, positions, sizes, text, grid lines, every digit and opaque original colors exactly. Export RGBA PNG with real alpha. No checkerboard. No new background. No glow.
